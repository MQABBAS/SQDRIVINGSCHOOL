Chadderton driving test centre: a learner's guide

Guide to Chadderton driving test centre (Oldham): who tests there, what the roads are like, skills to practise and how to book under the 2026 rules.

Updated 9 October 20262 min readBy SQ Driving School · Greater Manchester

Key takeaways

  • Chadderton serves Oldham — commonly learners from Oldham, Rochdale, Tameside.
  • Prepare for the types of road around the centre, not one memorised route.
  • Since 2026 you can normally change a booking twice, and only to nearby centres.
  • Check the official DVSA statistics for pass rates.

Who tests at Chadderton?

Chadderton serves Oldham and nearby parts of Rochdale and Tameside — Chadderton, Royton, Failsworth, Middleton and beyond. Learners from Tameside, Oldham, Rochdale commonly book here, but there's no catchment area for new bookings — you can book any centre on GOV.UK.

What the roads are like

Expect hills, busy main roads, roundabouts and roads near the A627(M) and M60.

Your exact route isn't published in advance, and examiners vary routes. The best preparation is to be comfortable with the types of road around the centre rather than memorising one route.

Skills to practise for Chadderton

  • Hill starts and gear/brake control
  • Main-road junctions
  • Roundabouts
  • Following road signs

Every one of these is tracked as a skill in the DriveSQ Student Portal, so you'll know when you're ready.

Booking a test at Chadderton

Book on GOV.UK. Under the 2026 booking rules you can normally change a booking twice, and a booked test can only move to one of the three nearest centres. If Chadderton has a long wait, compare Bredbury, Rochdale, Bury, Hyde.

Pass rates for every centre are published in the DVSA's official statistics. Figures quoted on other websites often come from different years and don't agree, so check the official data if you want to compare.

On the day

  • Bring your photocard provisional licence.
  • Arrive early — late arrivals can lose their test.
  • We provide the SQ car and a warm-up lesson around the area beforehand.
  • Read our test day checklist and the most common faults.

Frequently asked questions

Who usually takes their test at Chadderton?
Learners from Oldham, Rochdale, Tameside commonly book Chadderton, but you can book any centre.
Can I practise the Chadderton test routes?
Routes aren't published and examiners vary them. We practise the types of roads around the centre so you're ready for any route.
Where can I find the Chadderton pass rate?
In the DVSA's official driving test statistics, published on GOV.UK.
