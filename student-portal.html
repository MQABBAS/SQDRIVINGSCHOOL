For students

Everything you need to pass.

Live progress tracking

A competency ring and 23 practical skills, each rated Not Started, Introduced, Developing or Independent after every lesson.

Theory study library

Eight theory sections — road signs, rules of the road, hazards, alcohol & drugs, vehicle safety, eco-driving and first aid.

Mock theory tests

A full 50-question mock, timed at 57 minutes with a 43/50 pass mark — or Practice Mode with instant feedback.

Instructor messaging

A direct chat with your instructor, plus one-tap WhatsApp and call buttons.

Confirm & reschedule

Confirm attendance in one tap, or request a new time with a reason — no phone tag.

Test readiness checklist

Hours, manoeuvres, junctions, independent driving, theory and instructor sign-off — it ticks itself off.

Test countdowns

Set your theory and practical dates and two live countdowns appear on your home screen.

Achievements

Eight badges — First Lesson, 10 Hours, All Manoeuvres, Test Ready and more — to keep you motivated.

Works on any device

No app store needed. Add it to your home screen and it opens like a native app.

How it helps you pass

Why portal learners progress faster.

Learning to drive used to rely on memory and guesswork. The portal replaces both with information.

1

Clarity removes fear

Learners who can see their progress in black and white go into lessons calmer. Calm drivers make better decisions — and better decisions pass tests.

2

Revision between lessons

Each practical topic links to a written guide once your instructor has taught it, so lessons build on each other instead of starting from scratch.

3

No wasted lesson time

Your instructor opens your record before every lesson and picks up exactly where you left off.

4

Honest test timing

The readiness checklist means you book your test when the data says you're ready, not when you hope you are.

5

Theory sorted early

Unlimited mock theory tests with a section-by-section breakdown show exactly what to revise.
