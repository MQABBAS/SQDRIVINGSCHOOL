How long will it take to learn to drive?

Plan your hours, weekly lessons and test waiting time — and find out exactly when to book your practical test and what it will cost.

Key points

  • The DVSA average is 45 hours of lessons plus 22 hours of practice.
  • Driving more often shortens the calendar time without costing much more.
  • Book your test so your date lands when you'll be ready — the planner works it out.
  • Pass your theory test first; the certificate lasts two years.

"How long will it take me to pass?" is the question we hear most. The honest answer depends on how many hours you need, how often you drive and how long the test waiting list is. This planner puts all three together and tells you when to book your test — the bit most learners get wrong.

Where the 45 hours comes from

The DVSA says the average learner needs about 45 hours of professional lessons plus 22 hours of private practice to pass. It's an average across everyone — some people need 25 hours, some need 80. Things that usually shorten it: driving several times a week rather than once, private practice with family between lessons, and starting in an automatic. Things that lengthen it: long gaps between lessons, nerves (very manageable with the right instructor) and booking the test before you're ready.

Pace matters more than total hours

Try the planner with 2 hours a week and then 6 hours a week. The total cost barely changes, but you go from about half a year to two months. Frequent lessons mean less time re-learning what you'd forgotten. That's the idea behind intensive courses — and why we recommend 2-hour lessons (£70) over single hours for most learners.

When to book your practical test

You can only book your practical test after passing your theory test, and since 2026 you can normally only change a booking twice. Book too early and you risk turning up before you're ready; book too late and you finish lessons and then wait months. The planner subtracts your waiting time from your test-ready date to give a "book by" date. Check real dates on GOV.UK — waiting times differ a lot between centres. See our waiting times guide and 2026 booking rules.

Frequently asked questions

How many driving lessons do I need?
The DVSA average is about 45 hours of lessons plus 22 hours of private practice, but it varies a lot from person to person.
How long does it take to learn to drive?
At 2 hours a week, 45 hours takes around 23 weeks. At 6 hours a week, about 8 weeks. Test waiting times can add to this, so book at the right moment.
When should I book my driving test?
After passing your theory test, at a date that lands when you expect to be ready. Subtract the current waiting time from your expected test-ready date.
How much does it cost to learn to drive in Manchester?
At SQ's £35 an hour, 45 hours is £1,575 (less with block discounts), plus £23 for the theory test and £62 for a weekday practical test.
How long is a theory test certificate valid?
Two years. If you don't pass your practical test in that time, you have to take the theory test again.
