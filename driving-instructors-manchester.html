Looking for a driving instructor in Manchester? Instead of phoning round, tell us your postcode, gearbox and the times that suit you — SQ Driving School (part of DriveSQ) matches you with an instructor who covers your area, and you can see every lesson and skill in the free DriveSQ Student Portal.

How we match you with an instructor

  1. Your postcode — we look for instructors who already teach around you, so pick-ups are easy and you learn your local roads.
  2. Manual or automatic — and whether you'd like a female instructor.
  3. Your times — weekdays, evenings or weekends.
  4. Your test centre — so your lessons build towards West Didsbury, Cheetham Hill or wherever you'll test.

Not clicking with your instructor? Tell us and we'll find you another — no awkward conversations.

What to check about any driving instructor

  • The badge on the windscreen. A fully qualified Approved Driving Instructor (ADI) displays a green badge. A trainee instructor working under a trainee licence displays a pink one. Both are legal — but you should know which you're paying for.
  • Dual controls on the car.
  • A clear price with nothing hidden — ours is £35/hr, £40 for a single hour.
  • A plan — after your first lesson you should know roughly how many hours you need and what comes next.
  • Progress you can see — every SQ learner gets the DriveSQ Student Portal.

Questions worth asking before you book

  • Which test centre do you know best?
  • Do you teach in my area at the times I'm free?
  • Do you do 2-hour lessons? (Two hours means real progress each time.)
  • How will I know when I'm ready for my test?

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a good driving instructor in Manchester?
Check the windscreen badge (green for a fully qualified ADI, pink for a trainee), make sure the car has dual controls, get a clear price and ask how they'll track your progress. Or let us match you by postcode, gearbox and times.
Can I change instructor?
Yes. If it isn't working, tell us and we'll match you with someone else.
Are your instructors DVSA approved?
Instructors display their DVSA badge in the car — green for a qualified ADI, pink for a trainee under a trainee licence. Ask us which you'll have when we match you.
How much does a driving instructor cost in Manchester?
With SQ it's £35 an hour for lessons of two hours or more, £40 for a single hour, and £350 for 10 hours (£320 for NHS staff, students and M16/M18/M19).
