Automatic lessons FAQ

Questions from Edgeley learners.

How much are automatic driving lessons in Edgeley?
£70 for a 2-hour lesson (£35/hr), £40 for a single hour, or £350 for 10 hours — £320 for NHS staff and students.
Do automatic lessons cost more than manual?
No. SQ Driving School charges the same for manual and automatic lessons.
Can I drive a manual car if I pass in an automatic?
No — an automatic licence only covers automatic cars. You'd need to pass a manual test to drive manual cars.
Where will I take my automatic test from Edgeley?
Most Edgeley learners test at Bredbury or West Didsbury. We'll take you in an SQ automatic car with a warm-up lesson first.
