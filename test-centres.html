Booking your test

How to book your own test.

Learners book their own practical tests on GOV.UK. Here's how we help.

You need your theory test pass before you can book your practical.

Get ready with your instructor

3

Book on GOV.UK

Book your own practical test on the official GOV.UK service — never pay a third party for a slot.

4

Choose your centre

Waiting times vary between centres. Check a few nearby centres on GOV.UK.

5

Tell us your date

Message us your test date and centre so we can plan lessons and test-route practice.

Test day

What to bring.

  • Your UK provisional driving licence (photocard)
  • Glasses or contact lenses if you need them — you'll read a number plate from 20 metres
  • An SQ car — we can arrange the car and a warm-up lesson before your test
  • Arrive early — late arrivals may lose their test
