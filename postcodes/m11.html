Learning to drive in M11

The M11 district covers Openshaw, Clayton, Beswick in Manchester. We pick up from homes, schools, colleges and workplaces right across the district.

Expect a gradual build-up: calm streets near home first, then the roads that matter for Manchester drivers — city-centre one-way systems, bus lanes, trams on shared streets, the Mancunian Way and busy multi-lane roundabouts — plus quieter residential roads to build confidence first.

Most learners from M11 take their practical test at Cheetham Hill or West Didsbury. Read our test centre guides.

Local tips

Driving tips for M11 learners.

🚲

Cyclists

Leave at least 1.5 metres when overtaking cyclists at up to 30mph.

🛑

Box junctions

Don't enter a yellow box unless your exit is clear.

🏫

School streets

Expect children, parked cars and lollipop crossings around school times.

🌙

Dark evenings

Dip your headlights for oncoming traffic and watch for pedestrians in dark clothing.
