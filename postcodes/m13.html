Learning to drive in M13

The M13 district covers Ardwick, Victoria Park, Chorlton-on-Medlock in Manchester. We pick up from homes, schools, colleges and workplaces right across the district.

Lessons start on quieter local streets and build up to the roads Manchester learners meet every day — city-centre one-way systems, bus lanes, trams on shared streets, the Mancunian Way and busy multi-lane roundabouts — plus quieter residential roads to build confidence first.

Most learners from M13 take their practical test at Cheetham Hill or West Didsbury. Read our test centre guides.

Local tips

Driving tips for M13 learners.

🔄

Big roundabouts

Follow the lane markings; they override the general left/right rule.

🧭

Independent driving

Practise following sat-nav and road signs — about 20 minutes of the test is independent.

🅿️

Parked cars

Look for feet under parked cars and doors opening as you pass.

🚌

Bus lanes

Check the times on the blue signs — many bus lanes are only active at peak hours.
