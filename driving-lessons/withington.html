Learning to drive around Withington

Learners in Withington get to know Manchester's roads step by step. Expect city-centre one-way systems, bus lanes, trams on shared streets, the Mancunian Way and busy multi-lane roundabouts — plus quieter residential roads to build confidence first.

We begin somewhere calm near Withington and only head for busier roads when you're ready. Every new skill is introduced slowly and practised until it clicks. Before your test we'll practise around Cheetham Hill and West Didsbury test centres.

Local driving tips

Tips for driving around Withington.

🚶

Turning into side roads

Give way to pedestrians crossing or waiting to cross the road you're turning into.

🚑

Emergency vehicles

Stay calm and only move over when it's safe and legal.

🌧️

Rain

Double your gap in the wet — Manchester's roads get slippery fast.

🚋

Trams

Never stop on tram tracks, and watch for trams on shared roads.
