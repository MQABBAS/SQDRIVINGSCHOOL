Sale driving test routes: roads, junctions and mistakes

In-depth guide to Sale driving test centre (M33 7ER): the roads on reported test routes, tricky junctions, local landmarks, the pass rate and the mistakes learners make.

Key points

  • Sale test centre: 36–38 Poplar Grove, Sale M33 7ER.
  • Pass rate 49.1% (2024-25, about 10,000 tests).
  • Reported routes use roads like Cross Street and Washway Road.
  • DVSA doesn't publish routes — prepare for the road types, not one route.

Sale test centre is on Poplar Grove, a short walk from Sale town centre and close to Brooklands. It serves almost all of Trafford and parts of south Manchester. Routes typically leave along Cross Street, the A56 through the middle of Sale, so the first few minutes of every test are on one of the busiest roads in the borough.

Where it is: 36–38 Poplar Grove, Sale M33 7ER. Check the address on your booking confirmation and on GOV.UK before you travel — centres occasionally move.

Pass rate at Sale

49.1%

Sale car test pass rate — 2024-25, about 10,000 tests.

That's 0.6 points above the Great Britain average of about 48.5% for the same period. Source: passrates.uk — Sale driving test centre. Different websites publish slightly different figures for different periods; the official numbers are in the DVSA's DVSA1203 statistics.

A pass rate tells you about the roads and the candidates, not about your chances. What moves your odds most is being genuinely test-ready. Our pass-rate study turns pass rates into expected attempts and costs.

The roads around Sale test centre

Expect the A56 corridor (Cross Street, Washway Road, Chester Road) with bus lanes and changing speed limits, and then classic suburban Trafford: long residential roads with parked cars, school zones, mini-roundabouts and crossings. Independent driving often follows signs, for example towards Sale Water Park.

The DVSA no longer publishes official test routes, and examiners vary them. The roads below are the ones reported most often on recent routes from this centre, cross-checked between several public route sheets and local instructors. Your test could use any of them, or none.

Mistakes learners make at Sale

These are the faults that the roads around Sale make most likely. They line up with the DVSA's national list of the most common reasons for failing — see each one explained.

Driving in a bus lane during its hours

Washway Road's bus lanes have set times. Entering one when it's in operation is a fault — and a fine after you pass.

Read the blue sign for times every time; if in doubt, stay out.

Moving off too early at junctions

Emerging onto the A56 or Dane Road before a safe gap, forcing others to slow.

Wait until you're sure. Undue hesitation is a smaller risk than pulling out in front of someone.

Speed control on residential roads

Driving at 30 where parked cars and school entrances call for much less.

Match your speed to what you can see, not the sign.

Missed speed-limit changes

Not noticing a change in limit along the A56.

Scan for repeater signs and street lights; adjust smoothly before the change, not after.

Hesitation at roundabouts on the way to Sale Water Park

Stopping at clear roundabouts during independent driving.

Look right early on approach so you know whether you can keep moving.

Who tests at Sale?

Learners from Sale, Ashton upon Mersey, Brooklands, Timperley, Altrincham, Stretford, Urmston, Flixton, Partington and parts of Chorlton and Wythenshawe commonly book Sale. There's no catchment area for new bookings, so you can book any centre — but since June 2026 a booked test can only move to one of the three centres nearest it. Frequently asked questions

Where is Sale driving test centre?
36–38 Poplar Grove, Sale M33 7ER.
What is the pass rate at Sale test centre?
49.1% (2024-25, about 10,000 tests), against a Great Britain average of about 48.5%.
What roads are on the Sale driving test routes?
Commonly reported roads include Cross Street, Washway Road, Chester Road, Dane Road, Marsland Road. The DVSA doesn't publish official routes and examiners vary them.
Can I practise the Sale test routes with SQ?
Yes. We teach on the roads around Sale test centre and finish with a mock test and a warm-up lesson that ends at the centre.
Are driving test routes published?
No. The DVSA stopped publishing test routes, so any route you see online has been reported by learners or instructors.
