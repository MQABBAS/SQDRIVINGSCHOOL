Free prize draw

Win a £35 lesson voucher.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?
Yes. Entering costs nothing and you don't have to buy lessons. Sending a booking request with the box ticked is enough.
Do I have to tick the box?
Only if you want to enter. If you leave it unticked we'll still help you book — you just won't be in the draw.
When is the draw?
Entries close at 6pm (UK time) on the last day of each month. The winner is usually chosen the same evening or within a few days.
What can I spend the voucher on?
£35 off driving lessons with SQ Driving School (part of DriveSQ). It's valid for 6 months.
Can I enter more than once?
One entry per mobile number each month. You can enter again next month.
Will my name be shown?
Only if you say yes. If you agree, we show your first name and postcode area — for example “Aisha from M19”.

Prize draw terms

Promoter. SQ Driving School, part of DriveSQ Driving School, Manchester. Contact us on WhatsApp or phone: 07352 932003.

Prize. Each month's prize is a £35 driving lesson voucher, to use towards lessons with SQ Driving School / DriveSQ. This draw is run together with DriveSQ's monthly Free Hour Draw, so all eligible entries from both go into the same draw.

Who can enter. UK residents aged 17 or over who live in an area we cover. DriveSQ and SQ Driving School staff, instructors and their households can't enter.

How to enter. Send a booking request using the “Book in 60 seconds” form on this website and tick the box to enter the draw. Entry is free — no purchase is necessary and you don't have to book or pay for lessons to win. One entry per person (per mobile number) per month; extra entries are ignored.

Closing time. Entries for each month close at 6pm UK time on the last day of that month. If you enter after that month's draw has taken place, you're entered into the next month's draw.

Choosing the winner. One winner is chosen at random from all eligible entries for the month, shortly after entries close. We keep a record of every draw. Entering is a chance to win, not a guaranteed prize.

Telling the winner. We'll contact the winner by WhatsApp, text or phone within 7 days of the draw. If we can't reach the winner within 14 days, or they aren't eligible, we may choose another winner.

Using the voucher. The voucher can only be used for SQ Driving School / DriveSQ driving lessons and is valid for 6 months. There's no cash alternative, and it can't be transferred, sold or exchanged. It can be used alongside our other offers.

Publicity. We'll only show a winner's name on our website or social media if they agree, and then only their first name and postcode area.

Your data. We use your details to run the draw, contact the winner and reply to your booking request. Entering doesn't sign you up for marketing. See our privacy policy.

Changes. We may change or end the draw for future months by updating this page. This won't affect entries already made or vouchers already given.

Law. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
