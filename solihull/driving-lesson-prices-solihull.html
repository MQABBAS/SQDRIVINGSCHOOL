What does learning to drive cost in Solihull?

Using the DVSA's average of about 45 hours of lessons, that's around £1,575 at £35/hr — less with blocks — plus the £23 theory test and £62 weekday practical test (£75 evenings, weekends and bank holidays). Use our timeline planner for your own numbers.

How Solihull prices compare

We looked at advertised prices from driving schools and national providers covering Solihull in October 2026. Advertised single-hour rates mostly clustered around £32–£36, with a range of roughly £27–£42; 10-hour blocks ranged from about £300 to £410. Some providers don't publish a price at all, and some add a booking fee. Check exactly what's included before you compare.
