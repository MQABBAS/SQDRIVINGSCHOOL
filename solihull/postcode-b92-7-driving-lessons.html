The good and the hard here

Good: Easy places for parallel parking practice. Calm residential roads for junction practice.

Harder parts learners meet:

  • Emerging from side roads with parked cars blocking your view.
  • Cyclists and delivery vans stopping suddenly.
  • Speed humps and 20 mph limits near schools.

A test fault at South Yardley we prepare B92 7 learners for: Lane choice on the A45 — Ending in the wrong lane at junctions. Read gantry and lane signs early.

What lessons look like in B92 7

Olton: Olton's tree-lined residential roads suit first lessons; the A41 Warwick Road and Lode Lane give busy main-road practice, and shift-change traffic around the JLR plant is real-world anticipation training.

Landmarks you'll drive past: Olton station, JLR Solihull plant (Lode Lane), Olton Mere.
