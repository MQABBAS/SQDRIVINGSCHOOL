About Lyndon School

An 11–16 school near Hobs Moat Road, whose junction with Ulleries Road topped the council's 2016–18 collision rankings. It's an 11–16 school, so most learners start lessons after moving on to college or sixth form.

Roads around Lyndon School

  • Daylesford Road
  • Hobs Moat Road

Solihull is rolling out 20mph limits at every school location (the first order took effect on 28 August 2025). Driving past schools at home time is excellent hazard-perception training.

When to start

  1. Apply for your provisional licence at 15 years 9 months

    So it arrives in time for your 17th birthday.

  2. Start theory early

    The DriveSQ Student Portal includes a theory library and 50-question mock tests.

  3. First lesson on your 17th birthday

    We pick up from school, home or college.

  4. Test around exams

    Most students test at South Yardley (41.6% pass rate in 2024-25). Avoid booking during exam season.
