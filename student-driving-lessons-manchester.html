Key facts, checked

  • Provisional licence: £34 online — apply from 15 years 9 months.
  • Theory test: £23. Practical test: £62 on weekdays, £75 evenings, weekends and bank holidays.
  • National car test pass rate: 48.7% in 2024-25 (DVSA data).
  • Instructors: ADIs (green badge) and PDIs (trainee, pink badge) — always disclosed before you book.
  • Our prices: see lesson prices — manual and automatic are the same.

Sources: GOV.UK provisional licence · GOV.UK test fees · DVSA test data by centre · passrates.uk national figure
