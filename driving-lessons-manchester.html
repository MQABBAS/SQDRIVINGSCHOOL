Manchester driving test centres — pass rates and routes

Most Manchester learners test at West Didsbury (M21, 50.9% in 2024-25) or Cheetham Hill (M8, 43.7%). Sale, Bredbury and Chadderton are close enough that many people book them for an earlier date. Each centre has its own roads and its own common mistakes — we practise on them before your test.

What learning to drive in Manchester is really like

  • Trams and bus lanes. Metrolink runs on shared streets in the city centre, and bus lanes and bus gates are enforced — driving with trams · bus lanes and gates.
  • Big multi-lane roundabouts — Kingsway, Princess Parkway and the Mancunian Way junctions reward early lane choice. Roundabout guide →
  • Quiet roads first. Every learner starts somewhere calm (Levenshulme, Burnage and Chorlton have plenty) before busy roads.
  • Motorways once you're ready. The M60 and M56 are minutes away for motorway lessons with dual controls.
  • Clean Air Zone: Greater Manchester's plans changed — what it means for learners.

Frequently asked questions

How much are driving lessons in Manchester?
With SQ Driving School it's £35 an hour for lessons of two hours or more, £40 for a single hour, £350 for 10 hours, or £320 for 10 hours for NHS staff, students and learners in M16, M18 and M19. Manual and automatic cost the same.
How many driving lessons will I need?
DVSA says learners need about 45 hours of professional lessons on average, plus private practice. Some people need fewer, some more — use the planner on this page for an estimate, and your instructor will give you an honest view after your first lesson.
Which driving test centre is best in Manchester?
Most Manchester learners use West Didsbury (50.9% in 2024-25) or Cheetham Hill (43.7%). Pick the one you can practise around and get a date at — preparation on that centre's roads matters more than its pass rate.
Do you pick up from home, work or college?
Yes — door-to-door pick-up anywhere we cover in Manchester, including the city centre, universities and hospitals.
Do you do automatic driving lessons in Manchester?
Yes, at the same price as manual. Automatic is popular in Manchester's stop-start traffic. See automatic driving lessons Manchester.
Can I have lessons in the evening or at weekends?
Many of our instructors teach evenings and weekends. Tell us your times when you book and we'll match you with someone who fits them.
Can I ask for a female driving instructor?
Yes. Say so when you book and we'll match you with a female instructor where one covers your postcode — and tell you straight away if one isn't free.
Is SQ Driving School part of DriveSQ?
Yes — SQ Driving School is a brand owned and managed by DriveSQ, which is why every learner gets the free DriveSQ Student Portal.
