FAQ

Quick answers.

How much are driving lessons?
A 2-hour lesson is £70 (£35/hr). A single 1-hour session is £40. A 10-hour block is £350, or £320 for NHS staff, students and learners in M16, M18 and M19.
Why are lessons 2 hours?
Two hours gives you time to settle in, learn a new skill and practise it properly. Learners progress faster with longer lessons. If you only need one hour, it's £40.
Do you teach manual and automatic?
Yes — both, across all of Greater Manchester.
What is the DriveSQ Student Portal?
A free online app every SQ learner gets. It tracks your progress, has a theory library and mock tests, and lets you message your instructor.
Who owns SQ Driving School?
SQ Driving School is a brand owned and managed by DriveSQ.
