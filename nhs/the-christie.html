Lessons that fit around shifts

Hospital rotas don't fit neatly into weekly 9–5 slots. Tell us your shift pattern and we'll plan 2-hour lessons around it — before an early, after a late, or on your days off.

We can pick you up from near The Christie in Withington, or from home. Lessons build up to the roads Manchester drivers use every day: city-centre one-way systems, bus lanes, trams on shared streets, the Mancunian Way and busy multi-lane roundabouts — plus quieter residential roads to build confidence first.

NHS FAQ

Questions from The Christie staff.

Do you offer an NHS discount for The Christie staff?
Yes — NHS staff get 10 hours of driving lessons for £320 instead of £350. Show your NHS ID badge, NHS email or a recent payslip.
Can lessons fit around my shifts?
Yes. Message us your shift pattern on WhatsApp and we'll plan lessons around early, late and night shifts where we can.
Can you pick me up from The Christie?
Yes — we can pick up from near the hospital in Withington, or from your home anywhere in Greater Manchester.
Where would I take my test?
Learners in Manchester usually test at Cheetham Hill or West Didsbury.

SQ Driving School is an independent driving school and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Christie or the NHS. The NHS discount is our own offer for NHS staff.
